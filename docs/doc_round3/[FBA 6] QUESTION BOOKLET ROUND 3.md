**OBJECTIVES** 

This round challenges contestants to demonstrate creative problem-solving, stakeholder-focused thinking, and compelling business storytelling, while sharpening the ability to think quickly and defend ideas. 

***Round 3A:*** Teams will step into the role of Product Owners, using the company selection from Round 2 to design a concrete, data-driven product, service or campaign that directly addresses identified customer needs and market gaps. This round tests the ability to turn analysis into innovative, stakeholder-focused strategies aligned with Vietnam’s banking/fintech and economic context 

***Round 3B:*** All four teams engage in a live debate format (1-on-1 debate). Teams can choose to support and develop their statement or challenge the opponent team on a surprise mini case related to the Vietnamese banking/fintech industry. This round tests critical thinking, quick responsiveness, clarity of argumentation and the ability to apply broad knowledge in a fast-paced, competitive environment

**NOTE** 

During 5 minutes of preparation, each team will discuss and prepare opening argument point. Teams do not have to choose support/affirmative or oppose/negative side. Both teams can be on the same side, yet each team has to prove your stance why they are better than the other team. If two teams have opposing stances, teams may point out why your opponent’s arguments are wrong/inappropriate/not fully developed and show your team’s stance.

All arguments should be grounded in data and real evidence while proving your points (citing direct references verbally during arguments). 

**RULES** 

Total time: \~1 hour long. Each sub-round lasts 20 minutes (main debate \+ judges’ feedback/questions) with a visible countdown timer on screen. Judges’ feedback/questions are ONLY ALLOWED after the main debate of each round. 

There will be 3 mini cases in total, corresponding to 3 sub-rounds For each sub-round, two teams will face each other and have 5 minutes to prepare the opening arguments in advance. The total debate time for each round is 8 minutes. Each team has 2 minutes to argue and then switches to the opponent team, repeating until the 8 minutes end. No team may respond twice in a row 

The Organizing Committee will toss a coin to decide which team will argue first 

Contestants must ensure inclusive, professional language; no personal attacks and no interrupting the other team’s turn 

**NOTE:** The time per sub-round can be changed and updated to teams if
